# Confirmed-adult eligibility

- Chile: exact reported age; include valid integer ages of 18 or older.
- France and Brazil: include published age-band codes 2 through 7; exclude
  code 1 because the 10–19 band cannot distinguish minors from ages 18–19.
- Missing, invalid, or ambiguous ages are excluded.

The public release contains cohort counts and aggregate analytical outputs, not
participant identifiers.
